- Add `sunFromEnvironmentMap(map)` and `keyLightFromHdri(map, bounds)` to the `@codepawlhq/67js/addons/hdri` addon (issue #555). They find the sun in an HDR map as a luminance-weighted centre, honour `rotation`, and return `lightDirection`, `keyColor`, `keyIntensity` and a `shadowBounds` view aimed along the sun over the scene bounds, or `null` for an overcast map. On Poly Haven san_giuseppe_bridge_4k with rotation pi the direction is within 0.05 degrees of the hand-tuned one. Core size does not change.
