- `scene_walkmap` and `walkMap` mark obstacles and keep clear of them (issue #559). `keepOut` (metres) with an optional `streetY` casts a down ray from 2.6 m above the street, marks a cell as an obstacle when its first hit is over 0.3 m above the lowest surface under it (kerbs stay walkable), closes one-cell seams, cuts the obstacles back out and clears every cell closer than `keepOut`; in code `walkMap({ obstacles })` and the new `walkableMask` do the same. `scene_walkmap` also works on recipe scenes, and `assetId` queries a catalog GLB above the 1,000,000 vertex render cap without rendering it (parsed in the server, textures dropped, up to 6,000,000 triangles). On the Bistro sample a 0.25 m grid with `keepOut` 1.0 takes 38 s and agrees with a Blender walk map on 99.95 percent of cells; see `docs/scene-query.md`.
