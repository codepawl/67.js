- Render a `scene.json` world along a camera track with `render-media` (issue #554). `--scene file --config scene.json` loads the same GLB, sky, renderer options, exposure and frame effects the showcase page reads, headless, and `--camera keys.json` (`{ "fps": 24, "frames": [[eye, target], ...] }`, or `createCameraPath` keys) drives the camera one frame per output frame through the existing bounded frame queue and encoder. Asset paths such as `/showcase/bistro/scene.glb` resolve under `--root`, then next to the config. The config's `renderer.memoryBudgetMiB` is the default budget (up to 8192 MiB with `--memory`). On an RTX 3060 the Bistro walk renders 120 frames at 960x540 in 13 s including load, 9.2 fps, against about 0.7 fps for the hidden-pane showcase capture.
