- scene:agent-scene: the core grows by about 150 bytes gzip for three call sites (the effects getter hands sources the scene clock, the compile step checks followed nodes, and the capabilities line names the steps), all of which call into the lazy post steps module. The shock ring and flash are new capabilities that no recipe could express before, and a recipe that does not name them loads none of their code.
- scene:agent-scene+lazy: the lazy total grows by about 3.5 KB gzip for `src/post-air.js` (the two steps, their shaders and their validation) and the two motion track gains. The module loads only when a recipe names `distortion` or `flash`, so no existing scene pays for it.
- goal:agent-scene: the core is over its goal and may only shrink; the extra bytes are the hooks named above, kept to the minimum that lets the steps follow the scene clock and a node without loading their code up front.
