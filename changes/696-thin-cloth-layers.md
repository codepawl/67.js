- A cloth sheet with `selfCollision: true` now reads its own `thickness` as the contact radius (never past half the shortest edge), so a flag dropped to the floor lies as a thin crumpled layer. Behaviour change: a sheet that sets both `selfCollision: true` and `thickness` used to ignore the thickness (radius 0.9 of half the shortest edge) and now keeps layers `2 x thickness` apart, so its baked frames differ; pass `selfCollision: { radius }` to keep a fixed radius. Sheets that set no thickness keep the old radius (#696)
