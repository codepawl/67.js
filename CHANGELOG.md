# Changelog

## 0.19.0

- `scene_review`, `scene_render_status` and `scene_render_result` return only what an agent acts on by default; `response_format: "detailed"` keeps the full diagnostics, digests and metrics, and `scene_capabilities` takes `concise` to drop the field notes of a topic (#656).
- `scene_capabilities` topic `examples` (and `examples.<tool>`) serves one valid call each for `scene_create`, `scene_apply`, `scene_create_asset` and `scene_render_submit`, with no growth of any tool schema (#657)
- MCP: one server holds up to 4 open scenes under a 96 MiB geometry cap; the least recently used scene is evicted with a clear error. `sessionId` (returned by `scene_create`) picks the scene on every tool, and a call without it uses the last used scene. Each scene keeps 16 checkpoints, oldest evicted first (`evictedCheckpointIds`), and a render job stays tied to its scene (#658).
- Motion drivers read custom properties on nodes and bones (`motion.properties`), distance and rotation difference variables, and can drive lights and shape keys (#694)
- Mesh particle emitters take `from: "edges"`, with an optional `group` and `sharpAngle`, so sparks spread along a rim by edge length (#695)
- Mesh particle emitters take a vertex group (`emitter.group`) that weights surface and vertex emission, so sparks come off a painted rim (#695)
- A cloth sheet with `selfCollision: true` now reads its own `thickness` as the contact radius (never past half the shortest edge), so a flag dropped to the floor lies as a thin crumpled layer. Behaviour change: a sheet that sets both `selfCollision: true` and `thickness` used to ignore the thickness (radius 0.9 of half the shortest edge) and now keeps layers `2 x thickness` apart, so its baked frames differ; pass `selfCollision: { radius }` to keep a fixed radius. Sheets that set no thickness keep the old radius (#696)
- A cloth sheet takes `softBody` (shape matching and pressure on a closed mesh), `selfCollision` and a `stiffnessGroup` vertex group, all baked and seekable (#696)
- Field expressions read face area, edge angle, island index and neighbour counts, and accumulate, statistic, blur, sample index and rank over a whole mesh domain (#699)
- A recipe curve takes `grow`, morph targets of the curve grown step by step, so a motion morph track grows a vine and seeking equals playing (#701)
- `mesh_edit` gains `unsubdivide` and `circularize`, and poke, dual, tris to quads and symmetrize now have Blender golden cases (#703).
- `effects.composite` keys a green screen (chroma) or a luma range out of the frame and draws it over a colour or gradient background, with the same `mask` as the other post steps (part of #707)
- Post steps take a `mask` by part names or a depth range, and `effects.stylize` adds sharpen, kuwahara, posterize and pixelate filters (part of #707)
- Mesh edits gain geometry masks: vertex_group measure curvature, edge or occlusion, and vertex_color gradients along a group, so a mesh with no UVs can carry edge wear and dirt (#708)
- Materials gain `wear`: a vertex group blends the material from paint to bare metal per pixel and a second group adds dirt, so a mesh with no UVs shows metal on its edges and dirt in its cavities (#708)
- Material graphs get `brick`, `whiteNoise`, `coord` and `mapping` nodes, so a procedural material works on a mesh without UVs (#708)
- Blended and transmissive parts cast sun shadows weighted by opacity and tinted by colour, so stained glass, curtains and leaf canopies throw coloured and partial shadows; `castShadow: false` turns it off (#709)
- `environment.localVolumes` adds up to 8 box or sphere volumes that absorb and emit light, in the rasterizer and the CPU path tracer (#710)
- A mesh boolean `cutter` can be `{ "geometry": "<id>" }`, another recipe geometry, so cuts form a bounded DAG with cycle and missing-ID errors (#730)
- Render jobs take a `wireframe` pass that draws polygon edges over the shaded surface and colours boundary, non-manifold, flipped and degenerate edges for visual review (#732)
- `exportObj` (OBJ with MTL text) and `exportStl` (binary STL) in the new `addons/export-mesh` entry write the same parts as `exportGlb`, in world space, with deterministic bytes (#747)
- The jolt adapter builds convexHull colliders from the flat xyz point array and frees its world when setup throws.
- `67js/addons/physics-jolt` adds `joltAdapter`, a second engine for the live physics runtime built on the optional `jolt-physics` package: the same contact and sensor events as Rapier on the same scene, queries, kinematic control and force fields, with recorded 500-box step times for both engines (part of #771)

- `67js/addons/player` adds vehicle and free-flight controllers, orbit, chase and side-scroller camera rigs, `createCameraClip` (camera pull-in against walls and ground), and a registry (`registerController`, `registerCameraRig`, `replaceController`, `replaceCameraRig`) for custom kinds and runtime swaps (#772)
- Fix: the vehicle and flight controllers ignore a zero-length step, so the runtime's first variable-mode tick no longer turns their speed and velocity into NaN.
- `67js/addons/player` adds a character controller (walk, run, jump, kinematic collide-and-slide on a ground plane, heightfield or boxes, with a `mover` seam for physics) and swappable follow, first-person and top-down camera rigs as runtime systems (#772)

## 0.18.0

- Clip layers on an imported asset take a `strip` with start, scale, repeat, blend in and out, extend and blend mode, so a walk repeated three times can crossfade into an idle (#693)
- Particle emitters take points from a mesh surface (weighted by area), its vertices or its closed volume, push them along the normal, and draw each particle as one of up to 8 listed nodes with a random or surface-aligned rotation (#695)
- `scene_apply` takes `bake-motion`: it samples nodes over a range into keyframe tracks (tracks, IK, constraints, drivers, ragdoll and rigid bodies), optionally drops keys within a position and angle tolerance, and removes the sources it replaced, so the motion exports as a clip (#697)
- `sdf` geometry meshes a tree of sphere, box, round box, capsule, cylinder and torus shapes with union, subtract, intersect, smooth blends and offset by surface nets, so blobby bodies and rounded joins need no hand modeling (#700)
- `curve` geometry takes `kind` poly or nurbs, several `splines`, arc, spiral and star `shape`s and a `trim`, so rails, ropes and a growing vine need no hand-placed points (#701)
- `uv_project` (cube, cylinder, sphere) and `uv_pack` mesh edits write and pack UVs with one texel density (#702)
- `mesh_edit` gains `poke`, `dual`, `tris_to_quads`, `symmetrize`, `scale_elements` and `extrude_edges` (#703)
- Add a `build` mesh modifier that reveals faces over `start` and `length` frames in index, seeded random or axis order, with `reverse` (#704).
- `scene_restore` takes `revision` to undo the last few `scene_apply` edits in one call, with no checkpoint made first (#744)
- Docs: add an FAQ page for the questions from the launch: three.js, size and speed, tokens, what the agent sees, undo and diffs (#745).
- Set `SIXTYSEVENJS_MCP_LOG=<file>` to log each MCP tool call (tool, duration, ok, result size, never arguments), and run `scripts/summarize-mcp-log.mjs` for calls, error rate and median duration per tool (#746)
- Depth precision by default (#751, phase 1 of 2): the colour pass draws with reversed-Z and a 32-bit float depth buffer wherever `EXT_clip_control` exists, at any far / near ratio, from the first frame; the 16-bit depth buffer is gone; without the extension the depth buffer is 24-bit and the near plane fits the visible bounds when the camera left near unset; `reversedDepth: false` opts out; `installDepthPrecision`, `hasFarOverlap` and `requestReversedDepth` are removed (use `installCoplanarDiagnostics` for the warning); `bench/flicker/index.html` is a viewer with a flicker probe
- Flush faces stop flickering by default (#751, phase 2, step 1 of 2): faces that lie on one plane or a few millimetres apart, inside one mesh or across parts, are ranked per triangle and drawn with a small view-space depth bias, so the face in front wins at every distance and on every depth format; agent scenes do it unless `environment.resolveCoplanar` is `false`, a renderer does it with `resolveCoplanar: resolveFlushParts`; `decal` and `depthLayer` use the same bias; what cannot be ranked (more than 15 faces stacked on one plane, double-sided faces a gap apart) comes back as a `coplanar-overlap` warning with a `reason`; `coplanarLayers`, `resolveCoplanarParts` and `EXACT_FLUSH_DISTANCE` are removed; `bench/flicker` has trap scenes (`?trap=`, `probe.mjs --traps`)
- Hidden flush faces are cut away (#751, phase 2, step 2 of 2): where an opaque face covers another on exactly the same plane (1 cm2 or more), the covered part is cut out of the copy the hidden face draws with, every vertex array interpolated, so a stack of any depth on one plane stops flickering and no longer reports `rank-overflow`; parts and their geometry stay as they are (`flushOrderOf(part).geometry` holds the cut copy), instances of one geometry share a cut, and the path tracer traces the cut geometry; a face under a blended or masked face, or a double-sided face under a one-sided one, is still ranked; `bench/flicker` gains the `plaque-closeup-0.5m` trap with an open-pixel count
- Near-flush faces are cut too (#751): an opaque face in front of another by at most a thousandth of the side of their shared area (from 1 mm to 1 cm) cuts the face behind it, as a flush face does, instead of taking a rank; the Melbourne city model, whose stacked blocks nest facades 5 to 17 mm apart up to 17 deep, now reports no `rank-overflow`
- scene_inspect and scene_capabilities take the inputs Haiku sent in the bench: short mesh edit branch names (meshEdit.inset), dotted fields (environment.spotLights), near topic names (uvs, scene.terrain), source nodes without a mesh of their own, includeBounds with kind hierarchy; an unwalkable path start names the nearest walkable point (#761)
- scene_inspect with a node ID lists the parts below it and tags each with `fromNode`; a node with no part below it, such as a skeleton joint, returns its transform (parent, joint flag, local translation, rotation and scale, rest world position) instead of an error (#761)
- `67js/addons/runtime` adds `createRuntime`, a live game loop with fixed, variable or interpolated steps, ordered pluggable systems, pause on a hidden tab and a frame-time readout (#770)

## 0.17.0

- bake.gridFog: the sun the fog scatters is dimmed by the fog between it and the sun (#543)
- bake.gridFog: true (with irradianceGrid) lets the irradiance grid scatter in the scene's volume fog, off by default, for sunlit closed rooms with fog (#543)
- An irradiance grid bake runs on the kernel worker with its 90 s limit instead of the page's 20 s, so 8 grid bounces fit a hall like temple-hall (#543)
- Volume fog: opt-in `scatterOctaves`, `multiScatter`, `multiExtinction`, `multiAnisotropy`, `keyTransmittance` and `surfaceTransmittance` approximate multiple scattering of the sun in the haze and dim the sun by the haze between it and a point or surface. Off by default; the scenes without them render the same (#543)
- The publish workflow waits up to five minutes for the registry to serve a new version instead of two. The 0.16.0 publish succeeded, but the registry took longer than two minutes, so the check failed and skipped the release page.
- `applyAnimation(time, clip, { checks: false })` and `renderer.updateParts(parts, { checks: false })` skip the per-frame pose checks for clips you trust, and frames on the same clip no longer reset and snapshot every node. Eight animated characters: update CPU 1.6 ms to 1.3 ms with checks, 0.9 ms without.
- Skinned character frames cost less CPU: palette math reads joint matrices in place and a normal pose allocates nothing, so eight animated characters update about 0.1 ms faster (#572)
- Animating an imported glTF character costs about a third of the CPU it did: `applyAnimation` prepares the pose once instead of twice, reuses its rollback snapshot, and skin validation checks each distinct set of joint weights once instead of every vertex. Eight RobotExpressive characters went from 4.5 ms to about 1.5 ms per frame.
- Add a per-frame density and velocity `field` to volume fog (also `renderer.setFogField`), flow-advected noise (`flowStrength`, `flowPeriod`), a `profile` choice, and scattering from up to 8 point and spot lights in the fog (issue #573). A fog without a field or lights draws exactly as before.
- Breaking: a renderer that draws `environment.volumeFog` needs `import '@codepawlhq/67js/addons/fog-shader'` first (recipes, the film and the MCP server load it on their own), which takes about 2.5 KB gzip out of the core (#577)
- Motion blur and bloom no longer leave a ghost of the previous frame after a seek (#578)
- A few mirrored skin vertices (a finger pinched between two joints) no longer fail the frame. They draw pinched, and `renderer.diagnostics` lists `skin-reflected-vertices` with the count and the joints and weights of the first one (#581)
- Materials accept `alphaMode: 'add'` for additive glow, and the opacity of blend and add parts changes each frame through `updateParts` without a new batch. (#586)
- Volume fog takes `detailOctaves`, `detailAmount`, `warp` and `contrast` for wispier mist, `profileSoftness` for where a layer top starts to fade, and `keyScattering` to scale only the key light shafts (#586)
- Metal with detailed normal and roughness maps no longer sparkles from frame to frame at grazing angles; `environment.specularAntiAliasing` sets the strength (#588)
- resolveCoplanar: resolveFlushParts no longer layers pairs that lie on one plane to within a micrometre (the depth buffer already settles them) and settles chains of equal-sized parts, so it stops adding flicker on dense scenes (#593)
- A film with a very long lens (far / near over 10,000, such as near 0.08 and far 9000) now switches to reversed-Z float depth on its own: `Renderer67` loads `reversed-depth.js` as a separate chunk on the first such frame and every frame after it draws with it (`await renderer.reversedDepthReady` to have it from frame one; `reversedDepth: false` keeps the 24-bit buffer). On the metro film, 24-bit depth could not tell surfaces 2 micrometres apart at 2.6 m, and stairs, canopy panels, handrails and steel flickered between frames; those pairs went from thousands of flipping pixels to under 60 (#593)
- `resolveCoplanar` is now a function, `resolveFlushParts` from `67js/addons/coplanar-overlap`, so the plane-keyed search stays out of the core bundle. Its depth layers also work under reversed depth: the slope part of the offset is 16 times larger there, because the constant part moves a surface by under a micrometre in a float depth buffer (#593)
- motion.constraints: adds copy_transforms, limit_distance, limit_scale, stretch_to, locked_track, transformation and action, each one scene_motion call (#595)
- Recipes gain `cone`, `icoSphere`, `circle` (filled or ring) and `line` geometries, and `plane` is accepted as an alias of `grid` (#596)
- Fields can read other surfaces: `nearest` (distance, point or normal of the closest point on a surface) and `raycast` (hit, distance, point or normal along a direction, with a `miss` value), plus `attr: group` to read a vertex group back. In `instances` the surface is a geometry id, so a scatter can avoid a path mesh and follow terrain; in `set_position`, `displace`, `vertex_group` and `vertex_color` it is a geometry record, and `vertex_group` stores the result by name (#597)
- Force fields: a `fields` list on the recipe `particles` and `cloth` sections accelerates particles, hair and cloth through one shared evaluation, with wind, turbulence, vortex, drag and magnet types, `strength`, `falloff`, `radius` and `seed`. The same seed gives the same frames (#598).
- Ocean modifier: the `ocean` mesh edit and stack node turn a flat grid into a seeded sea (Phillips spectrum for the wind, `choppiness`, `size`, `seed`, `time`) and write the vertex group `foam` where the surface folds. The same seed and time give identical vertices. Part of #599.
- Per-object motion blur: `effects.motionBlur.objects` (0 to 1, off by default) smears each part that moves during the shutter along its own screen path, while the still background stays sharp; it replaces camera motion blur on that frame and loads on demand (#600)
- render jobs: passes now include `diffuse`, `glossy`, `shadow`, `ao` and `mist` next to albedo, normal, emission, depth and objectId; diffuse + glossy + emission add back to the lit image, and `exr` keeps values above 1 (#601)
- Three named post steps join defocus (`effects.depthOfField`) in the look: `effects.colorBalance` (`lift`, `gamma`, `gain`, each three numbers), `effects.glare` (`streaks`, `count`, `angle`, `fogGlow`, `threshold`, `size`) and `effects.lensDistortion` (`distortion`, `dispersion`). Each is off until a recipe names it and draws nothing at its neutral values, so a recipe without them renders the same pixels as before. Their code is a lazy module that loads only for a recipe that names one; `installPostSteps(renderer)` from `src/post-steps.js` adds the stages (#602)
- `scene_inspect` reports a `bake-stale` warning when motion has moved a light, the sun or a part off the pose a light bake was traced at; a scene without motion or without a bake pays nothing. `bake.rebake: true` bakes each pose a render or seek samples (the last 4 are kept, about 0.7 s each on a small room), so the baked light matches the frame and the warning does not apply. Material animation is not checked, see docs/light-bake.md (#603)
- MCP scene_create and set-environment now accept the 16 point, 8 spot and 7 area lights the engine renders, read from one limits file, not 8 point and 4 area (#604)
- Motion tracks animate lights, the sun, sky and fog, exposure and focus distance, so a sunset, a lamp flicker or a rack focus plays from a recipe (#605)
- Nodes take visible (keyable in a motion track), castShadow, receiveShadow, holdout and shadowCatcher, so films hide parts without parking them and renders composite over footage (#606)
- Scene cameras take projection orthographic with orthoScale, focalLength with sensorWidth (instead of fov), shift [x, y] and fStop for depth of field, in recipes and set-camera (#607)
- environment.hdri and a .cube environment.look: shipped HDRIs (sky-clouds, studio-softbox) and looks (teal-orange, noir), or catalog files by ID, work from recipes and MCP (#608)
- Material graphs: new outputs `opacity` (base texture alpha), `clearcoat` and `transmission` (per-pixel masks), and mixing nodes `layer` (mask-blended layers such as paint over rust), `hue` and `curve`. The rasterizer reads opacity and clearcoat, the CPU path tracer all three. Graphs without them bake the same (#609)
- A recipe mesh takes `shapeKeys` (named offsets or move-only edits), animated by the existing morph tracks and written as morph targets in a GLB (#610)
- Render jobs gain four optional output settings, all off by default so a plain job writes the same bytes: `transparent: true` (PNG with alpha, for `image` and `stills`), `format: "png16"` or `"exr"` (16-bit PNG and OpenEXR from a half-float frame, passes included: depth in metres as float32 `Z`, object IDs as float32 `id`), `region: { x, y, width, height }` (a crop of the whole frame, for `image`, `stills` and `video`) and `fps` up to 60. Cost is 16 blocker taps and 32 filter taps per lit pixel, only for a shadowed area light.
