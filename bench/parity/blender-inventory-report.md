## Suggestions

1. Add a `tabs` index to `scene_capabilities` that lists, in the Properties editor order (Render, Output, View Layer, Scene, World, Object, Modifiers, Particles, Physics, Constraints, Data, Material, Texture), the capability keys and the tool that edits each. `scene_capabilities` is a flat object of 67 keys; an agent that knows Blender looks for a tab, not a key name. The table above is a first draft of that index.
2. Offer Blender's terms as aliases in the index, without renaming keys: `meshModifiers` and `meshStack` under "Modifiers", `motionConstraints` under "Constraints", `viewTransform` under "Color Management", `renderPasses` under "View Layer". Stack operations without a Blender modifier name: uv_unwrap, uv_pack, set_position, smooth_by_angle, bounding_box, convex_hull (`subdivide` is Blender's Subdivision Surface; accept `subdivision_surface` and `subsurf` as input aliases).
3. Order `docs/guide.md` and the tool descriptions by the same tab order, and give each tab one line that names the Blender tab, so a search for "Physics tab" or "Modifiers tab" lands on the right page.
4. Keep Blender's identifiers where Crucible already matches them (weld, edge_split, simple_deform, smooth_by_angle, weighted_normal); constraints without a Blender name: none.

## Ranked shortlist of missing features

Ranked by value to agents that build scenes, films and renders in the browser; sizes are rough (S under a day, M a few days, L a week or more). Out of scope per docs/goal.md and left out: mouse editor, video sequencer, motion tracking, Grease Pencil, .blend files, Python API.

| # | Feature | Why | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Constraint batch: Copy Transforms, Limit Distance, Limit Scale, Stretch To, Locked Track, Transformation, Action | Rigs, cameras and props follow other parts without hand-made keyframes; 14 of 29 constraints have no ledger coverage yet (issue #58). | S each, M together |
| 2 | Mesh primitives: plane, cone, ico sphere, circle and line | Every agent asks for a plane or cone first; today it builds them from cylinder or lathe. | S |
| 3 | Sampling in fields: nearest surface, raycast, proximity, plus Capture and Store Named Attribute | Scatter that avoids paths, grows on terrain and keeps density maps is how agents dress a world; fields cannot read other surfaces. | M |
| 4 | Force fields: wind, turbulence, vortex, drag, magnet | Moves particles, hair and cloth in one call; the ledger row is missing (issue #63). | M |
| 5 | Ocean modifier | Water scenes have a shader and no moving surface; a spectrum displacement is a bounded, deterministic pass. | M |
| 6 | Object motion blur | Camera blur exists; fast props and characters in films still look frozen (ledger: Cameras). | M |
| 7 | Lighting passes: diffuse, glossy, shadow, AO, mist | Agents and compositing steps need separable lighting to grade; only albedo, normal, emission, depth and object ID exist. | M |
| 8 | Compositor subset: color balance, glare, lens distortion, vignette, defocus | Film grade without a graph; a short list of named post steps beats a node editor for agents. | M |
| 9 | Shader mixing: Mix and Add Shader, Emission, Toon BSDF as a bounded graph | Surfaces that blend two materials (wet over dry, painted over rust) need mixing, not just layers. | L |
| 10 | Curve operations: resample, fillet, fill, reverse, profile sweep with caps | Rails, pipes, cables and roads start as curves; only the sweep exists (issue #45). | M |
| 11 | Rotation and Matrix math in fields: align to vector, rotate, transform point | Instances need orientation along a normal or path; fields hold position only. | S to M |
| 12 | Soft body | Jelly, cushions and flags; cloth covers flags, so this ranks last (issue #62). | L |
